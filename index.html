MY APPROACH

Digital Marketing Is More Than Running Ads

A successful digital presence is not built from a single channel.

Website → Traffic → Measurement → Data → Optimization → Growth

This is how I approach Full Stack Digital Marketing: understanding and connecting the different layers of digital marketing instead of treating them as separate activities.

From building the digital foundation to acquiring users, measuring their behavior, and improving performance, every layer should work together as one connected system.
